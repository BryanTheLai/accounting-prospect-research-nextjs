## 5. The 6 Automated Campaign Angles & Playbooks

### Campaign 1: SG-MY Subsidiaries (1.8K Prospect Pool)
- **Target Audience:** Managing Directors and Owners of Singapore SMEs with an operational Sdn Bhd subsidiary in Malaysia.
- **Pain Point:** Paying SGD $1,000–$2,000/mo to a Singapore firm or contemplating hiring a full-time RM 3,500/mo accountant in Malaysia for just 5 to 15 transactions a month.
- **Subject Lines:**
  - `quick question re: {{companyName}}'s Johor subsidiary books`
  - `accounting for {{companyName}} (Malaysia branch)`
- **Core Hook:** "Are you currently paying a Singapore firm in SGD—or maintaining a full-time salary in Malaysia—just to reconcile a handful of Malaysian expenses and file bi-monthly SST?"
- **Offer:** Flat RM 800 to RM 1,200/mo managed accounting reviewed by certified ACCA / CA Malaysia accountants.

### Campaign 2: JS-SEZ Operators (1.4K Prospect Pool)
- **Target Audience:** General Managers, Plant Heads, and Directors of factories in Senai, Pasir Gudang, Ulu Tiram, and Kulai.
- **Pain Point:** High clerical turnover in industrial parks, difficulty retaining accounting staff, and rising compliance demands from the Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone.
- **Subject Lines:**
  - `handling {{companyName}}'s factory accounts in Senai / JB`
  - `e-invoicing + bookkeeping for {{companyName}}'s Pasir Gudang plant`
- **Core Hook:** "With cross-border traffic and JS-SEZ expansion, finding and keeping reliable accounts clerks in Johor has become a headache. We handle end-to-end bookkeeping, SST, and payroll remotely with chartered accountant sign-off."
- **Offer:** Done-for-you factory bookkeeping directly integrated with AutoCount / SQL Account / Xero.

### Campaign 3: e-Invoice SMEs (2.5K Prospect Pool)
- **Target Audience:** Owners and Managing Directors of Malaysian trading, engineering, and logistics companies (RM 5M–RM 50M revenue).
- **Pain Point:** Panic over the phased LHDN e-invoicing mandate, disorganized paper invoice backlogs, and fear of penalties.
- **Subject Lines:**
  - `LHDN e-invoicing readiness for {{companyName}}`
  - `quick check re: {{companyName}}'s e-invoice transition`
- **Core Hook:** "Is your accounts team fully set up to transmit validated e-invoices to MyInvois for every transaction, or are you still relying on manual paper entries?"
- **Offer:** Free 15-minute e-invoicing gap audit + seamless migration support across AutoCount, SQL Account, Bukku, or Xero.

### Campaign 4: Xero Adopters (2.2K Prospect Pool)
- **Target Audience:** Singapore and Australian tech startups, agencies, and e-commerce companies with Malaysian entities using Xero.
- **Pain Point:** Local Malaysian bookkeepers don't know how to use Xero properly, or try to force migration to outdated desktop legacy tools.
- **Subject Lines:**
  - `Xero bookkeeping for {{companyName}}'s Malaysian entity`
  - `your Xero ledger + MY statutory filing`
- **Core Hook:** "Since you already run on Xero, you don't need a separate software stack in Malaysia. Our certified Xero advisors reconcile your Malaysian transactions directly in your existing ledger and handle local SST/LHDN filings."
- **Offer:** Plug-and-play Xero subsidiary management starting at RM 800/mo.

### Campaign 5: Shared Services Hubs (800 Prospect Pool)
- **Target Audience:** Country Managers and Operations Directors of overseas firms with remote engineering or customer support hubs in Kuala Lumpur / Penang.
- **Pain Point:** Complex monthly payroll withholdings (EPF, SOCSO, EIS, PCB, HRDF) and statutory compliance without a dedicated local HR/Finance team.
- **Subject Lines:**
  - `Malaysian payroll + statutory compliance for {{companyName}}`
  - `streamlining {{companyName}}'s KL team expenses`
- **Core Hook:** "Managing payroll withholdings, PCB tax deductions, and statutory compliance for a remote Malaysian team shouldn't require hiring an internal finance manager."
- **Offer:** Combined payroll, expense reconciliation, and statutory annual reporting in one low flat retainer.

### Campaign 6: Australia-Malaysia SMEs (1.1K Prospect Pool)
- **Target Audience:** Directors and Founders of Australian companies (Perth, Sydney, Melbourne) running back-office operations or contractors in Malaysia.
- **Pain Point:** Paying $50 to $80 AUD/hour for Australian bookkeepers when the work involves identical UTC+8 operations in Malaysia.
- **Subject Lines:**
  - `re: {{companyName}}'s Malaysian back-office accounting`
  - `Perth-Malaysia accounting desk for {{companyName}}`
