## 1. Executive Summary & Outbound Thesis

When targeting SME Managing Directors and business owners in Singapore and Malaysia:
1. **Founder Cold Calling is 10x More Capital/Time Efficient Than Sales Rep Calling:** Founder-to-founder authority achieves a **13%–15% connect rate** and a **15% connect-to-meeting set rate** (vs 5.4% and 4.6% for junior SDRs).
2. **Cold Email is Volume-Leveraged; Cold Calling is Conversion-Leveraged:**
   - Closing 3 clients via Top-Quartile Cold Email requires **~2,200 emails** sent across 3–4 domains and 10 warmed mailboxes.
   - Closing 3 clients via Top-Quartile Founder Calling requires **~1,058 direct mobile dials** (~350 dials per client).
3. **Voice AI (Bland, Vapi, Retell, ElevenLabs) Fails in Singapore & Malaysia:**
   - Latency (>1,000ms cross-border SIP routing) causes instant hangups.
   - Unrecognized foreign robotic voices trigger IMDA / SPF anti-scam hostility.
   - Singlish / Manglish code-switching cannot be handled naturally.
   - **Verdict:** Do NOT use Voice AI for cold outbound to SG/MY business owners.
4. **The Winning Dual-Track Playbook:**
   - **Track 1 (Asynchronous Engine):** 500 cold emails/day via Smartlead + secondary M365 domains for wide net coverage (2–3 replies/day).
   - **Track 2 (Founder Sniper Sprint):** 30 minutes/day dialing 15 high-conviction mobile leads directly from the Streamlit cockpit, followed immediately by 1-click WhatsApp messages.

## 4. Cold Call Time Economics & Dialing Realities

```
[Dial Initiated]
       │
       ├─► 72% No Answer / Voicemail: 35 – 45 sec cycle
       ├─► 15% Gatekeeper Screen:     60 – 120 sec cycle
       └─► 13% Connect:
                 ├─► 85% Quick Brush-off: 30 – 90 sec
                 └─► 15% Booked Discovery: 5 min 50 sec – 7 min 30 sec
```

*Throughput Baselines:*
- **Manual Dialing (Solo Founder using mobile + Streamlit Cockpit):** **20 to 28 dials / hour** (accounts for CRM context viewing, dialing, ringing, and 2–3 conversations).
- **Single-Line Power Dialer (e.g. Close / PhoneBurner):** **45 to 60 dials / hour**.
- **Multi-Line Predictive Dialer (3+ numbers):** **80 to 120 dials / hour**. *(Never use for B2B MDs: introduces 1–2 sec transfer lag and causes immediate disconnects).*

## 6. The 30-Minute/Day "Sniper Outbound" Protocol

A solo founder cannot afford low-yield spray-and-pray outbound. 30 minutes per day must be treated as a precision high-leverage block.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       DAILY 30-MINUTE POWER SPRINT                          │
│                                                                             │
│  [00:00 - 22:00]  THE 15-DIAL SPRINT                                        │
│                   • Dial 15 curated direct mobiles from Streamlit cockpit   │
│                   • Yield: 1.5 - 2 live connects                            │
│                   • Goal: Pitch -> 10-min Zoom / coffee discovery           │
│                                                                             │
│  [22:00 - 30:00]  THE OMNICHANNEL "TRIPLE TOUCH" (8 Mins)                   │
│                   • 1-Click WhatsApp to no-answers & busy prospects         │
│                   • LinkedIn connection request with 1-line note            │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Daily Execution Steps:
1. **Calling Windows:** 10:00 AM – 11:30 AM or 4:00 PM – 5:15 PM SGT.
2. **The 45-Second Founder Pitch:**
   > *"Hi [Name], Bryan here, founder of Outbounder. Look, I know I called you out of the blue, but do you have 30 seconds, or did I catch you in a meeting?"*  
   > *(Wait for reply — 80% say "I have 30 seconds, what's this about?")*  
   > *"I run an accounting desk specifically for Singapore companies operating Johor factories and subsidiaries. Most owners tell us paying SGD rates or hiring a full-time RM3.5k accountant in JB for just 5 to 10 invoices a month is wasteful. We handle the entire bookkeeping, SST, and audit prep with certified ACCAs for RM800/mo. Not selling today—just wanted to see if this is on your radar, and if so, grab 10 minutes on Zoom next Tuesday?"*
3. **The 8-Minute WhatsApp Follow-up (Primary Deal Channel in SG/MY):**
   - Click the WhatsApp link in the Streamlit app for every prospect who did not answer or asked to send info:
   > *"Hi [Name], Bryan here, founder of Outbounder. Just tried your mobile regarding {{companyName}}'s Johor subsidiary books. Dropped you a quick note on LinkedIn as well. If this is relevant, happy to share our 1-page pricing sheet here. Cheers."*
4. **Milestone Roadmap to 3 Closed Clients:**
   - 15 dials/day = 75 dials/week = 300 dials/month.
   - Monthly output: ~36 live founder conversations $\to$ 5.4 booked discovery calls $\to$ 1 signed retainer per month.
   - **Time to 3 signed clients:** ~10 to 12 weeks of consistent 30-min/day execution.
